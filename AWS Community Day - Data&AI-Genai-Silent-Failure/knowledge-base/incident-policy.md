# Critical Incident Management Policy

## SEV-1 incidents

A SEV-1 incident is an incident that causes a complete outage or critical degradation of a customer-facing service.

For SEV-1 incidents affecting payment processing:

- Customer-facing functionality must be restored within 30 minutes.
- The incident must be escalated after 10 minutes if recovery has not been achieved.
- The Incident Commander owns the recovery process.
- A post-incident review must be completed within 48 hours.

## SEV-2 incidents

SEV-2 incidents must restore customer-facing functionality within 4 hours.

The incident must be escalated after 60 minutes.
